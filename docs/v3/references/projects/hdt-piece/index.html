HDT Systems: the stage piece

The approved design for HDT Systems' screen on the Projects stage (concept B of the Cowork study, "Control rings"). The card plays the sequence as an 11-second loop so it can be watched; the site plays it once, as the preview below shows. Port sceneControls, fillMaterial, stageWipe and stageFrame from the module script. The layout and copy around them are a sketch.

Frames

Control rings

The company's Google Workspace as a rack of servers, with four rings around it, one for each of ISO/IEC 27001's control themes, each carrying a person. The rings swing loose, then one by one settle into line, turn gold and lock. The servers' lights go steady, and gold flows up from the rings into a shield above them.

Tells
Securing the workspace, with controls working toward ISO/IEC 27001. The strongest single image at small sizes.
Labels
None. The picture carries no text and no tag.
Loop
11 seconds here. On the stage it plays once as HDT comes into view, then holds the shield: see the preview below.

B on the Projects stage

Scroll inside the box. As HDT Systems' title rises, its screen wipes up over Bukhait Sefrar's, the piece rises with the wipe, and the sequence starts halfway through it. It plays once, then holds the locked rings and the shield, gently moving, while the chapter is read. Scroll back up past it and it plays again.
