What changes from step 05

Arcs

Each arc is a 3D tube along the great circle from Muscat. It arches higher the farther it goes, so even the short hop to the UAE lifts, and the sphere hides any part that passes behind it. The head draws out over 1.5 s, the tail follows it in, and a ring ripples out where it lands.

Sphere

A lit Windsor sphere replaces the near-black disc. 11,061 land dots sit on an even lattice about 1° apart and fade toward the edge. The graticule keeps step 05’s 30° grid.

Turn

Spin is step 05’s motion: the real Earth’s direction, one turn every 110 s, so the arcs wait while Muscat is round the back. Sway rocks slowly between 62° and 100° east, which keeps Muscat and most places in view, so the arcs don’t stop.
