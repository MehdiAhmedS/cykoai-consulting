PART 01

The diagnosis

Nothing in the current build is individually wrong — it is the complete default kit of 2025–26 AI-generated "premium dark tech" sites: navy ground, gold gradient text, glass cards, glow blobs, tilt-on-hover, magnetic buttons, a custom cursor, film grain, and a rotating 3D crystal. Together they are a fingerprint. Worse, they contradict the rules your own design system already wrote down:

THE CURRENT BUILD DOES

KEEP — IT'S ALREADY GOOD

  • The section architecture. Hero → What we do → Services → Why → Philosophy → Process → Contact is the right story order.
  • The stack. Next.js + Lenis + GSAP ScrollTrigger is exactly the right toolset for the new direction.
  • Two structural patterns. The pinned Philosophy sequence and the horizontal Process scroll — keep the mechanics, redress the surfaces.

  • Glass, glows, gradient text, grain — all four banned by your own system.

  • Generic abstract glyphs — replace with the diagonal mark and ring geometry, or nothing.
  • Details eroding trust: section numbers out of order (03/04 swapped vs. page order); Testimonials & CaseStudies components built but unused — never fake proof, publish thinking instead.
PART 02

Principles — the page must be the proof

Cykoai sells calm in a time of flux. A site dressed in glow and hype contradicts the pitch before a word is read. Every design decision should demonstrate the promise:

P1

Calm is the demo

Generous whitespace, slow confident motion, one accent color. If a section feels busy, it is arguing against the business.

P2

The koi, abstracted

Agility as behavior, not imagery: fluid smooth-scroll, layered drift, interfaces that respond instantly but never jump.

P3

The sequoia, abstracted

Endurance as structure: strict grid, hairline rules, concentric ring geometry, vertical scale. Things that look like they were built to stay.

P4

One diagonal, everywhere

The logo's cut is the entire graphic language — section dividers, reveals, underlines, hover states. One motif, repeated with discipline, beats ten decorations.

P5

Proof over pitch

Artifacts instead of adjectives: engagement notes, methodology, numbers, published thinking. Consulting is bought on evidence of judgment.

P6

Light body, dark bands

White "memo" surfaces carry the reading; Windsor Blue bands carry the cinema — hero, philosophy, footer. The alternation is the rhythm of the whole page.

PART 03

The recipe

Concrete rules for the rebuild — your existing tokens, applied with more nerve.

TYPE
Space Grotesk display at -0.03em / 1.02 leading, pushed larger than feels safe (hero ≈ clamp(2.9rem, 6.5vw, 5.8rem)). Inter body at 1.125rem / 1.65, max 46–52ch. JetBrains Mono 10–11px caps with .14–.22em tracking for every label, number, and annotation — the "instrumentation" voice.
COLOR
White / #F9F9FA surfaces; Windsor Blue bands for nav, hero, philosophy, footer only. Driftwood is spent like money: links, rules, focus rings, one highlighted word per screen — never a fill. Spruce and Burnt Rose only inside tags and data. Zero gradients.
LAYOUT
1320px max grid, hairline 1px borders instead of shadows, radii 2–8px and nothing rounder. Asymmetry over centering: big statement left, evidence right. Numbered everything (01–07) in mono.
MOTION
Lenis smooth-scroll under GSAP ScrollTrigger. Max 3 parallax speeds per viewport (≈ 0.85 / 0.92 / 1.0), max 2 pinned moments per page (Philosophy, Process). Reveals 500–800ms on expo-out, staggers ≤ 90ms. Hovers 200–300ms, color/rule shifts only — nothing scales, tilts, or follows the mouse. prefers-reduced-motion → fully static. Transforms and opacity only.
IMAGERY
None until it's real. The graphic layer is line geometry only: the diagonal at architectural scale, concentric rings, hairline grids. When photography arrives, treat it like Watershed's dithered landscapes — atmospheric, never stock.
VOICE
Sentence case everywhere. Short declaratives. Numbers over adjectives. No exclamation points, no "unlock/supercharge/revolutionary," no emoji. The register of a firm that has already thought this through.

PART 06

Motion technique cheat sheet

Everything below is DOM + CSS + GSAP — no WebGL anywhere.

T1 — FOUNDATION

Lenis + ScrollTrigger

Lenis drives scroll; lenis.on('scroll', ScrollTrigger.update) syncs GSAP. Everything else hangs off this. Already in your dependencies.

T2 — DEPTH

Layered speed planes

Elements carry data-speed="0.85–1.0"; one rAF loop translates them. Three planes max per viewport (Locomotive pattern, demoed in all three mockups).

T3 — REVEALS

Masked lines & drawn rules

Headlines reveal line-by-line from overflow:hidden wrappers; hairlines and diagonals draw in via stroke-dashoffset / scaleX (Exo Ape / basement).

T4 — TRANSITIONS

The diagonal wipe

Zentry's clip-path move at your 45°: clip-path: polygon(…) scrubbed by scroll to hand off light→dark bands. Use once or twice, not everywhere.

T5 — PINNED MOMENTS

Philosophy & Process only

Two pins per page maximum. Philosophy: text scrub (keep yours). Process: horizontal track with a Driftwood progress rule. Every pin needs a reduced-motion static fallback.

T6 — PERFORMANCE

The calm contract

Transforms/opacity only; will-change sparingly; IntersectionObserver gates everything offscreen; 60fps or the effect gets cut. A janky calm site is a contradiction.

KEEP — next · lenis · gsap · framer-motion (or fold into GSAP) · tailwind DROP — three · @react-three/fiber · @react-three/drei · @react-three/postprocessing · postprocessing
